01The Forest

You are a hunter in a procedurally generated forest that never repeats. Every swing is weighted by your tools, your skill, and the season you happen to be playing in.

Common gems can be bought. Rare gems and above are hunt-only — there is no path that converts money directly into a Diamond find, and that is deliberate. The moment the best gem could simply be purchased, hunting would stop being a game.

02How It Works

Off-chain hunting

Movement, swings, and finds resolve instantly in your browser. No waiting, and the chain is not in the animation loop.

Committed in advance

Every possible outcome is committed as a Merkle root before a season starts, so a result cannot be rewritten after you see it.

Ranked by efficiency

Seasons last 14 days and rank return per ETH spent. One Diamond on a small stake outranks a thousand Common gems hoarded.

Gems are burned

Redeeming burns a fee to the treasury and destroys the rest. The treasury holds ETH backing every outstanding gem, publicly.

Tools carry you

Four tiers, each with durability. Tools break, and repairs are paid for in gems — so gear is earned through play.

Skill is not for sale

Gems burned raise the ceiling on what you can find. Money raises your balance; only play raises what you can hunt.

03$DEEPWOOD

$DEEPWOOD is the settlement layer for the game economy.

Redemption pays in ETH until the token rail is switched on, and in the token at identical face value afterwards. Same numbers either way — the token changes the unit, never the value of what you found.

Status: pre-launch. The game, its contracts, and the token are in testnet testing. Nothing here is live on mainnet, and no token is in circulation. Figures on this page describe the design, not a released economy.
